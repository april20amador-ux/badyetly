SYSTEM OVERVIEW

What is Badyetly?

ⓘ

Badyetly is a personal budget tracking web application designed to help users organize and monitor their financial activities. It provides tools for recording income and expenses, setting budget limits, tracking savings goals, and viewing spending summaries.

Its goal is to make personal money management more organized, understandable, and convenient through a simple digital platform.

OUR PURPOSE

Purpose of the System

◇

Badyetly aims to help users become more aware of their spending habits and manage their available money. Through organized records and visual summaries, users can make more informed budgeting and saving decisions.

Private by Default Records stay on your device
Offline-Ready Installable PWA support

01 · Getting Started

Account & Home
01Account Setup, Recovery PIN & Backup Restore ›
Navigation: Login Screen → Create Account / Restore Backup
  1. Open Create Account, enter your account name, password (4+ characters), and an optional 4-digit Recovery PIN.
  2. To recover an existing profile, click Restore Backup on the login screen and select your .json file.
  3. Use Try Demo Account anytime to preview sample finances safely.
  1. TLPiliin ang Create Account, ilagay ang pangalan, password, at optional na 4-digit Recovery PIN.
  2. Kung may lumang .json backup file ka, i-click ang Restore Backup para maibalik agad ang iyong records.
02Home Dashboard & Balance Privacy ›
Navigation: Sidebar → Home
  1. Review Total Income, Total Expenses, and Total Balance filtered by All Time, This Month, or a custom month.
  2. Click Hide on the Balance card to mask figures as ₱••••• in public spaces.
  3. Use the date toolbar under Recent Transactions or tap Today for daily totals.
  1. TLMakikita sa Home ang kabuuang kita, gastos, at natitirang balance (All Time o This Month).
  2. Pindutin ang Hide para gawing ₱••••• ang balance kapag may ibang nakatingin.

02 · Daily Money Tracking

Income, Expenses & Bills
03Recording Income & Allowances ›
Navigation: Sidebar → Transactions → Add Income
  1. Enter the income source (e.g., Salary, Allowance), amount, and date.
  2. Click + Add Income. Entries can be edited or removed anytime from the list below.
  1. TLIlagay ang pinagmulan ng pera (hal. Sahod, Allowance), halaga, at petsa, tapos i-click ang + Add Income.
041-Tap Quick Chips, Smart Entry & #Tags ›
Navigation: Sidebar → Transactions → Smart Expense Entry
  1. Tap a Quick-Add Chip to log frequent items in a single click, or create custom chips via + Add Chip.
  2. Type multiple lines in Smart Entry (e.g., Rice - 50, Jeep - 25) for automatic keyword categorization.
  3. Append #CategoryName to override or assign custom categories manually.
  1. TLIsang pindot sa Quick-Add Chip para sa madalas na gastos, o mag-type ng maramihang linya gaya ng Kanin - 50 para kusang maikategorya.
  2. Maglagay ng #CategoryName sa dulo kung gusto mong ikaw ang pumili ng kategorya.
05Recurring Bills & Transaction Filtering ›
Navigation: Sidebar → Transactions → Recurring Bills
  1. Register fixed obligations (WiFi, Rent, Utilities) under Recurring Bills and click Log Payment monthly.
  2. Use the top search bar and category dropdown to filter historical transactions instantly.
  1. TLIlagay sa Recurring Bills ang regular na bayarin (WiFi, Renta, Kuryente) at i-click ang Log Payment kapag bayad na.

Analytics, Budgets & Goals

Navigation: Sidebar → Analytics

Navigation: Sidebar → Budget Planner
  1. Select an expense category, specify a monthly spending limit, and click + Save Budget.
  2. Switch months at the top to compare past spending against your target limits.
  1. TLPumili ng kategorya at ilagay ang budget limit bawat buwan. Kusang lalabas ang progress bar kung malapit ka nang lumagpas.
08Savings Goals & Target Pace Calculator ›
Navigation: Sidebar → Savings Goals
  1. Create multiple goals with an optional Target Date to view exact daily, weekly, and monthly saving pace.
  2. Enable Deduct this goal from Spendable Balance to separate saved funds from spendable cash on Home.
  1. TLMaglagay ng goal name, target amount, at optional Target Date para makita ang kailangang ipon araw-araw o buwan-buwan.

1. How do I create a Badyetly account?›

Click Create Account on the login screen, enter your account name and password (4+ characters), confirm it, and optionally add a 4-digit Recovery PIN.

TLI-click ang Create Account sa login page, ilagay ang pangalan, password, at optional na 4-digit Recovery PIN para sa proteksyon ng password reset.
2. What should I do if I cannot log in or forgot my password?›

Verify your exact Account Name (tap Saved Accounts if shown) and password. Click Forgot password? on the login screen and enter your 4-digit Recovery PIN if configured.

TLSiguraduhing tama ang Account Name at password. Gamitin ang Forgot password? at ilagay ang 4-digit Recovery PIN kung nag-set ka nito.
3. Why did my saved account disappear after clearing browser data?›

Local profiles are stored in your browser's localStorage. Clearing site data, using Private/Incognito mode, or switching browsers removes local storage. Click Restore Backup on the login screen and upload your .json file to recover everything.

TLNawawala ang local account kapag nag-clear ng browser cache, naka-Incognito, o lumipat ng browser. I-click ang Restore Backup sa login screen at piliin ang .json backup file mo para maibalik lahat ng records.
4. What is the Demo Account used for?›

Click Try Demo Account on the login screen to explore sample transactions, analytics, and goals without affecting your personal records.

TLPang-testing ang Demo Account na may sample data para masubukan ang app nang hindi nagagalaw ang tunay mong account.
5. How does automatic expense categorization work?›

Badyetly matches keywords in your description (such as rice, coffee, jeep, kuryente) to built-in categories. Add #CategoryName at the end of a line to assign a specific or custom category.

TLBinabasa ng Badyetly ang keywords sa gastos mo (hal. kanin, kape, pamasahe, kuryente). Pwede ring maglagay ng #CategoryName sa dulo para ikaw ang pumili ng kategorya.
6. Why was my expense line skipped or not added?›

Each line must include a valid positive amount, formatted like Rice - 50 or Coffee 120, one purchase per line. Lines without a positive number are ignored.

TLSiguraduhing may positibong halaga bawat linya (hal. Kanin - 50) at isang item lang bawat linya.
7. How do 1-Tap Quick-Add Chips work?›

Tap any chip under Smart Expense Entry or the mobile Quick Add modal to log recurring daily purchases immediately. Click + Add Chip to customize your list.

TLIsang tap lang sa Quick-Add Chip para ma-record agad ang madalas na gastos. Pindutin ang + Add Chip para magdagdag ng sariling shortcut.
8. Can I edit, delete, or track monthly recurring bills?›

Yes. Every income or expense row includes Edit and Delete actions. Fixed monthly bills (WiFi, Rent, Electricity) can be saved under Recurring Bills and logged with Log Payment.

TLOo, may Edit at Delete ang bawat record. Para sa buwanang bayarin (WiFi, Renta, Kuryente), ilista sa Recurring Bills at pindutin ang Log Payment buwan-buwan.
9. How do I set and monitor monthly budgets?›

Open Budget Planner, select a category and monthly cap, and click + Save Budget. Usage bars recalculate automatically as you log expenses for that month.

TLSa Budget Planner, pumili ng kategorya at ilagay ang limit bawat buwan. Kusang mag-a-update ang progress bar habang nagre-record ka ng gastos.
10. How do Goal Pace and Spendable Balance deduction work?›

Adding a Target Date calculates how much to set aside per day, week, or month. Checking Deduct this goal from Spendable Balance ring-fences saved funds from spendable cash on Home.

TLKapag may Target Date ang goal, lalabas ang kailangang ipon bawat araw, linggo, o buwan. I-check ang Deduct from Spendable Balance para nakahiwalay ang ipon sa panggastos sa Home.
11. What happens if I delete a savings goal?›

Only the selected savings goal is removed. All your income entries, expenses, and category budgets remain intact.

TLYung piniling savings goal lang ang mawawala. Ligtas at hindi mabubura ang mga kita, gastos, at budgets mo.
12. How do I back up my data or export CSV / PDF reports?›

Open Settings → Backup & Restore. Click Download Backup for a full .json restore archive, Export CSV for spreadsheets, or Print / Save PDF for monthly reports.

TLSa Settings, i-click ang Download Backup para sa buong .json file, Export CSV para sa Excel, o Print / Save PDF para sa buwanang summary.
13. How do I move my records to another browser or device?›

Export a .json backup on your current device and restore it on the new browser or device. Over https://, you can also sign in with Google Cloud Sync.

TLMag-download ng .json backup sa lumang device at i-restore sa bagong device o browser. Kung naka-https://, pwede ring gamitin ang Google Cloud Sync.
14. Why is Google Cloud Sync unavailable in file:// mode?›

Modern browsers restrict Google Sign-In popups on local file:// paths for security. Use Download Backup (.json) locally, or host/serve Badyetly over Live Server or https:// for Cloud Sync.

TLHinaharangan ng browser ang Google login popup kapag binuksan nang direkta mula sa folder (file://). Gamitin ang Download Backup (.json) o buksan sa Live Server / https:// para sa Cloud Sync.
15. How do I switch Light/Dark Mode or hide balance amounts?›

Use the Dark / Light toggle in the top bar or Settings. Click Hide on your Total Balance card (or set default privacy in Settings) to mask amounts as ₱•••••.

TLPindutin ang Dark / Light sa itaas o sa Settings para magpalit ng theme. I-click ang Hide sa Balance card para gawing ₱••••• ang halaga.
16. What is the difference between Clear All Data and Delete Account?›

Clear All Account Data wipes transactions, budgets, and goals while keeping your login credentials. Delete Current Account permanently removes the profile itself. Always download a backup first.

TLAng Clear All Account Data ay bubura ng lahat ng records pero mananatili ang account login. Ang Delete Current Account ay bubura pati ng mismong account. Mag-backup muna!
